1. Introduction

Welcome to Autovate Automations ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our automation infrastructure and AI consultation services.

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, and phone number when you book a consultation or use our auth system.

3. How We Use Data

We use your data strictly to provide, maintain, and improve our services. This includes engineering bespoke AI workflows, connecting external APIs on your behalf, providing customer support, and personalizing the Ankita AI consultation experience. We do not sell your personal data.

To provide our services, we integrate with third-party providers including Google (Firebase Auth, Gemini API), Calendly, OpenAI, Twilio, Stripe, and Make.com. These services have their own privacy protocols. We only share the minimum viable data required to execute your automated workflows.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, secure API key management, and robust Firebase backend rules to prevent unauthorized access. You retain total ownership of your proprietary data and external API keys integrated into our systems.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your session (e.g., Firebase Authentication status) and track website performance. You can disable cookies in your browser, though this may impact your ability to log in to our dashboard.
